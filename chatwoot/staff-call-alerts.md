# Call alerts: steps for staff

A visitor on the website can ask us to call them back. When that visitor phones in and your
GoTo line rings, Chatwoot shows a popup on your computer. Click it to see who is calling.

## Do this one time

Use Chrome or Edge on your computer.

1. Sign in to Chatwoot with your work email. Use the same email as in GoTo.
2. Click your name at the bottom left. Click **Profile settings**.
3. Go down to **Notification preferences**.
4. In the **Push notification** column, tick **You are mentioned in a conversation**.
5. Turn on **Enable push notifications for your browser**.
6. The browser asks to show notifications. Click **Allow**.

Do these steps again on each computer and each browser that you use.

## When your line rings

1. A popup shows: "You have been mentioned in conversation (#12)".
2. Click the popup. The conversation opens.
3. Read the yellow note. It shows the caller's phone number and their code.

The popup does not show the phone number. Open the conversation to see it.

## Find visitors who wait for a call

In the left menu, under **Labels**, click **callback**. Each conversation there waits for a call.

## Before you call a visitor back

1. Open the conversation.
2. Open the panel on the right. Under **Conversation Actions**, click **Assign to me**.
3. Then call the number in the yellow note. Use GoTo.

This tells the team that you have this visitor. Two people do not call the same visitor.

When you dial the number in GoTo, Chatwoot assigns the conversation to you. The conversation
stays in the **callback** list until you resolve it.

## After the call

- The visitor answered: when you are done, click **Resolve**. Chatwoot removes the **callback**
  label.
- Nobody answered, or voicemail answered: do not resolve. The visitor stays in the list.

## Save the visitor's phone number

Spirit does not save the number on the contact. Anyone can type any number in the chat.
When you know the number is correct, you can save it on the contact. Then, when that visitor
calls in, your line shows a popup for them.

## No popup?

- Keep the browser open. It can be minimised.
- Turn off "Do not disturb" on your computer.
- In the browser, click the icon left of the address. Set **Notifications** to **Allow**.
- Ask your Chatwoot admin to add you to the **Spirit Chat/Phone** inbox and to your team.
  Chatwoot tells only the members of that inbox.
