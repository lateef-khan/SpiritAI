---
name: one-unit
description: >-
  Use this when the question is about one specific machine — its service history, its work
  orders, its warranty — or about the machines one customer owns, before calling ask_unit.
  Covers reading an order number the person gives you.
---

An order number is 'ServiceId-OrderId'. A bare number with no dash is not a whole one.
Do not guess the missing half: pass it on as given, and if nothing comes back, say the
number looks incomplete and give the expected form.
