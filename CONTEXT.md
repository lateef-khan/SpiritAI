# SpiritAI

Spirit Fitness's staff and customer chat, plus the hub that puts the company's staff apps in one
window.

## Language

### Hub

**Hub**:
The one window staff open to reach every staff app, each app shown in its own frame. It is the
Spirit AI site's front page; the chat is one app inside it.
_Avoid_: shell, portal, launcher

**Desk**:
The staff inbox app (Chatwoot) as it appears in the Hub.
_Avoid_: Chatwoot (outside setup and code talk), inbox app

**CRM**:
The customer records app (Twenty) as it appears in the Hub.
_Avoid_: Twenty (outside setup and code talk)

**Person**:
A staff member who signs in to Spirit AI; the one identity every Hub app hangs off.
_Avoid_: user (say "Desk user" or "CRM user" for the other apps' records), account

**Linked user**:
A Desk user or CRM user that is recorded against a Person by its id in that app.
_Avoid_: matched user, mapped account

**Local sign-in**:
An app's own password login page, kept as a back door for when Spirit AI is down.
_Avoid_: break-glass login, admin login
